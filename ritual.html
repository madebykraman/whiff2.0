SPRAY.

Fragrance is not only what you smell. It is what happens after.

A way of wearing

Do not rush the first spray. Perfume is a moving composition. Give each stage enough time to become itself.

01 / SpraySkin.Wrists, neck, chest. Give the fragrance somewhere warm to live. Avoid rubbing it away.
02 / WaitTime.Do not judge the opening. Let the top notes move through you.
03 / LiveMove.The heart develops with heat, air and the life around you.
04 / RememberStay.What remains is often what becomes yours.

The first spray is an introduction.
The drydown is the memory.
