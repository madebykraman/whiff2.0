The feeling

Curiosity before certainty.

There is a particular pleasure in not knowing what waits on the other side. Ajaab begins with saffron and citrus brightness, lets incense and rose darken the centre, then leaves cold woods, amber and skin musk close to the body.

It is not loud. It is not immediately familiar. It is designed to make you lean in.

Not for

Anyone looking for a simple fresh scent or an obvious first impression.
