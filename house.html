WHIFF is a contemporary emotional fragrance house. We build scents around moments, not categories.

The idea

A fragrance can be a place you return to.

We started with a simple question: what does a feeling smell like?

Not masculine. Not feminine. Not simply woody, floral or fresh. Curiosity has a texture. Desire has a temperature. Distance has a trail.

Collection 01, STAGES, follows nine emotional states from the unfamiliar to surrender.

Every feeling has a scent. Every scent leaves a memory.
